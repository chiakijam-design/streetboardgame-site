Quick summary for younger users

  • Do not enter your real name, school, address, phone number, social-media ID, or password in a question or display name.
  • Joining a leaderboard is optional. Only participating names, scores, and ranks are shown to players in that quiz.
  • A creator may see answers through a secret management link. In LIVE games, viewers see their own answer and result.
  • A question is sent for publication only when the creator leaves the publication checkbox on. The operator may edit it before publishing.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. Streetboardgame does not store card numbers.
  • Ask a parent or guardian before using the Service if you are under 18.

This is a readable summary. The full policy below applies.

1. Scope and controller

This Policy applies to Streetboardgame. Tomoaki Hirakawa, operating as the Streetboardgame operator, is responsible for the handling described here. “Personal information” has the meaning given by Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

2. Information collected

  • Friend challenge: creator name, optional leaderboard display name, selected questions, answers, score, rank, leaderboard consent time, and creation/answer times.
  • Submitted questions: only questions and choices the user agrees to send for possible publication, source mode, submission time, moderation flags, and an IP hash used to prevent abuse.
  • Reports: the reported question, reason, optional details, report time, and an anti-abuse IP hash.
  • LIVE: game title, optional YouTube channel URL/ID/name and public video data, authorized public-video captions, questions, choices, schedule, player names, votes, answers, and room operation history.
  • Creator registration: identity/business and contact details, channel information, uploaded images, invitation status, Stripe Connect ID, revenue, refund, and payout records.
  • Purchases: product, price, time, Stripe payment/customer/receipt identifiers, email, and download status. Card numbers are not stored on the operator’s servers.
  • Contact and access: name, email, message, IP address, Cookie data, pages, referrer, device information, and error/security logs.

Google passwords, Google access/refresh tokens, card numbers, and security codes are not stored in the operator’s database.

3. Purposes

Information is used to create and run games; count votes; show results and optional leaderboards; let creators manage answers; review and moderate submitted questions; detect personal information and abusive content; handle reports; verify creator/channel ownership; generate paid images; process payments, refunds, revenue sharing, accounting, and tax records; answer inquiries; recover incidents; prevent abuse; and improve quality and security.

Draft answers and secret tokens may be stored in your browser to resume play. The operator does not provide user information for third-party product or service AI training and does not sell or license personal information or aggregated statistics. A material future change would require the notices and consent required by law.

4. What other users can see

  • Leaderboard: participation is optional. Only a participating player’s display name, score, and rank are shown to players in the same quiz.
  • Creator management: per-question answers may be shown to the creator who holds the secret management link.
  • LIVE: if vote counts are enabled, anonymous aggregate counts are shown. A viewer sees their own answer, match status, and result.
  • Submitted questions: only content submitted for possible publication may be edited, approved, and shown in the shared library.

Likely real names, school names, social-media IDs, phone numbers, and addresses are blocked from publication submission. Sexual content, bullying, appearance attacks, and discriminatory content are flagged for review.

5. YouTube API Services

Streetboardgame may use YouTube API Services but is not provided, sponsored, or endorsed by YouTube, Google, or their affiliates.

Public channel/video data may be used to create candidate questions. When a channel owner chooses Google authorization, captions from up to eight selected public videos from the past year may be processed to create relevant candidate questions. Access tokens are revoked after processing and login credentials and refresh tokens are not stored. The YouTube Terms and Google Privacy Policy apply. Users can revoke access in Google security settings.

7. Third-party disclosure

Personal data is not disclosed except with the user’s direction or consent; to necessary processors; as part of a business succession; in response to a lawful public-authority request; to protect life, safety, property, or rights where consent is difficult; or as otherwise permitted by law. Individual buyer information is not normally given to creators; only revenue/refund/distribution totals needed for settlement are shared. Information is not sold to advertisers, data brokers, or AI developers.

8. Aggregated statistics

Non-identifying aggregates such as play counts, usage, incidents, and popular questions may be used to improve the Service, plan capacity, prevent abuse, and rank question popularity. Display names, free text, individual answers, and IP addresses are removed or aggregated, and re-identification is not attempted.

9. Retention

  • Friend-challenge rooms, answers, leaderboard names, scores, and ranks: 30 days after creation.
  • LIVE player names, votes, and answers: 24 hours after the LIVE ends.
  • Submitted questions, moderation flags, and reports: as needed for review, publication, rights, safety, and abuse prevention.
  • Authorized public-video captions: 30 days.
  • Paid result images and buyer name/download data: 30 days; transaction, refund, and revenue records: 7 years.
  • Operations/security events: 180 days; GA4 user/event data: 14 months; contact messages: 12 months after resolution.

Expired D1/R2 data is deleted or anonymized by a recurring task. Required legal, dispute, refund, fraud, and incident records may be held longer when necessary.

10. Cookies and analytics

Cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage support game state, resuming, analytics, and quality improvement. Disabling them may break resume or participation features. GA4 receives page, referrer, device/browser, and interaction information; names, emails, question text, and answer content are not sent as GA4 event parameters.

11. Security

Measures include two-factor admin authentication, short sessions, separation of game and purchase databases, private object storage, Content Security Policy, encrypted transport, access separation, automated deletion, and log monitoring. Users must also protect secret links and codes.

12. Your rights

Requests for access, correction, addition, deletion, suspension, erasure, or stopping third-party provision can be sent through the contact form. Identity may be verified. Some records may need to be retained for law, transactions, safety, rights, or an active game; where legally possible, the reason for a refusal will be explained.

13. Minors

Users under 18 must obtain necessary guardian consent. Users aged 10–12 must be supervised. Guardians may request access, correction, suspension, or deletion for a child through the contact form after relationship verification.

14. International processing

Cloudflare, Stripe, Google, YouTube, Formspree, and other providers may process information outside Japan. The operator reviews public information, contracts, and safeguards and provides information or obtains consent where required by law.

15. Revisions, law, and language

Important changes will be announced before they take effect; fresh consent will be requested where required. Japanese law applies. This English page is a convenience translation. If it conflicts with the Japanese Privacy Policy, the Japanese version controls unless applicable law requires otherwise.
