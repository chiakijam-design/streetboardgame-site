♡ Concept

Know Me Quiz

You really get
You know me, right?

10 questions to spark conversation, beyond guessing answers.

A game where others guess your answers and checking them sparks your next conversation. The creator makes 10 questions and sends participants a joining URL.

♡ What we value

Try again as many times as it takes to understand each other
↻

You can guess the answers again

If you're concerned about your score, you can retry the same 10 questions before posting your result.

○

You choose whether to publish

Appearing on the understanding board is optional. Results are displayed in completion order, without ranking by score.

✎

Let checking answers open a conversation

Use the result image and answer review report to talk about why answers matched and what surprised you.
