Frequently asked questions

How would you like to create your Know Me Quiz?

Choose shared questions one at a time and register your own correct answer to complete all 10. Edit the question and 5 choices or create your own questions.

How many people can try?

Up to 50 people can join one Know Me Quiz. Participants open the join URL on their own phones and answer 10 questions.

Can I play for free?

Create quizzes, share join URLs and QR codes, compare all 10 answers, and view answer review reports for free.

Is my score always made public?

It is not published. Respondents choose whether to post on the understanding board. They can try the same 10 questions again before posting.
