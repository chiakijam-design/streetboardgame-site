Content and Likeness Guidelines

Simple summary for children

  • Use only questions, photos, illustrations, and logos you created or have permission to use.
  • Do not enter real names, school names, social media IDs, phone numbers, addresses, or other information identifying you or others.
  • You cannot post harmful questions, sexual content, bullying, attacks on appearance, or discriminatory expressions.
  • Questions with “Let others use this” enabled may be reviewed, edited, and added to the question library by the operator.

This is a simplified summary. The full Guidelines below take precedence for the formal details.

1. Covered content

These guidelines apply to the following content created, entered, sent, registered, shared, or reported by users in Streetboardgame's regular challenge mode, LIVE streaming challenge mode, and related features.

  • Question text, 5 choices, explanations, categories, and other question data
  • Creator names, streamer names, streaming account/channel names, participant display names, and understanding board comments
  • Photos, portraits, avatars, profile images, and logos registered by streamers using paid sales when image registration is provided
  • Free result cards, paid original high-resolution result images, and text used when sharing them

2. Permitted materials and rights verification

Users may use only materials they created, hold rights to, or have permission to display, edit, incorporate into result images, provide to buyers, and share on social media in this service. Public availability on the internet or social media alone does not permit unrestricted copying or reuse.

  • For photographs, check both the photographer's copyright and the likeness and privacy interests of the people pictured.
  • If illustrations, logos, characters, products, building interiors, co-performers, or similar elements appear, verify any necessary permissions from rights holders, organizations, and facilities.
  • When using fan art, stock materials, or AI-generated images, verify that the terms of the creation tool, original materials, commission, and distributor allow commercial use in this service, editing, inclusion in result images, and delivery to buyers.
  • When using images of minors or information identifying minors, obtain explicit consent from the minor and their parent or guardian and any other necessary parties. Images of children who are nude, in swimwear, bathing, or otherwise potentially sexual may not be registered.

3. Custom questions and submitting candidates for publication

Custom and edited questions can be used in regular and LIVE modes. If you finalize a question while “Let friends and others reuse this quiz” is ON, its text and choices are submitted to the operator for publication review. With it OFF, you can still create your quiz, but the question is not submitted for publication in the shared library.

Content submitted for publication review is checked and edited by the operator for safety, readability, consistent wording, duplicate prevention, and rights protection. If accepted, it is made available to other users in the shared question library for regular and LIVE modes. Submission does not guarantee publication; the operator decides whether to accept, edit, disable, or reject it.

4. License to the operator

Copyright and other rights in user content remain with users or original rights holders. Users grant the operator a free, non-exclusive license to use submitted or registered content as necessary to provide the service, run games, review and edit content, publish it in the shared library, generate/display/deliver free and paid result images and downloads, re-provide services, support users, investigate misuse, and respond to rights violations.

Images may be cropped, resized, have backgrounds processed, combined with text or decorations, or converted to other formats as needed for the purposes above. Questions and choices may be edited for safety, readability, consistency, and duplicate prevention.

This license does not include permission to sell user content to third parties or use it indefinitely to train generative AI unrelated to providing this service. The operator will not use content to imply that users endorse third-party products, services, or political views without their prior consent.

5. Personal information and safety review

If strings resembling real names, school names, social media IDs, phone numbers, or addresses are detected in question text or choices, the candidate is not saved and correction is requested. Possible sexual content, bullying, appearance attacks, or discrimination are recorded for operator review. Automatic detection is a supporting feature and cannot guarantee detection of all personal information, rights violations, or inappropriate expressions.

Do not enter real names, school names, contact details, secret URLs, or other information identifying or tracking yourself or third parties into creator names, streamer names, participant names, or free-text board comments. Publishing private-life information learned from others as questions or comments without their consent is also prohibited.

6. Prohibited content

  • Unauthorized reproduction, impersonation, or infringement of others' copyrights, trademarks, portrait/privacy interests, reputation, publicity rights, or other rights
  • Child sexual exploitation, explicit sexual expressions, revenge pornography, voyeuristic photography, or other non-consensual sexual images or expressions
  • Content intended to discriminate, threaten, defame, bully, attack someone's appearance, expose personal information, or harass
  • Content encouraging self-harm, dangerous acts, illegal activity, fraudulent payments, scams, or unauthorized account access
  • False endorsements, affiliations, histories, or streaming account/channel details that mislead streamers, viewers, or others
  • Other content with a reasonable risk of violating rights or harming users' safety, especially minors

7. Automatically generated candidates and candidates based on external information

If YouTube integration or other automatic generation features are provided, accuracy, rights clearance, and suitability are not guaranteed even for candidate questions based on public information or information the streamer authorized us to obtain. Before publishing or using them, the creator or streamer must check the question and all 5 choices and correct or remove misinformation, personal information, sensitive topics, and unauthorized reproduction.

Answer review reports are generated automatically within this service from matching and differing answers. Questions and answers are not currently sent to external AI services for this purpose.

8. Streamer images and result images

If streamer image registration is provided, images are stored privately and not used in LIVE screens, free previews, or paid result images until operator approval. Rejected images are deleted. Approved images may also be suspended, re-reviewed, or deleted following rights complaints, safety concerns, or reports.

Registered images and previews are generally deleted within 24 hours after the LIVE ends, and paid result images after 30 days. Information needed for refunds, rights infringements, fraud, legal retention, or disputes may be retained to the extent necessary. For detailed retention periods, seePrivacy Policywill be followed.

Users or buyers may save their own result cards/images for personal use and share them on social media or messaging apps. Reselling the images, extracting materials, making merchandise, using them as advertising materials, edits harming rights holders, or distributing them under another person's name are prohibited. Follow the sharing platform's terms too.

9. Reviews, reports, and immediate hiding

The operator reviews submitted questions and registered images and may reject, edit, disable, or decline images if violations or potential violations arise. Review or approval does not guarantee accuracy, legality, or rights clearance.

Published custom questions can be reported from the game screen. Reported questions are immediately hidden until review is complete, then republished, edited, disabled, or deleted following re-review. False reports and abuse of the reporting feature are prohibited.

10. Reporting rights violations and requesting removal

Copyright holders, people pictured, guardians, and other rights holders,Contact formSubmit the relevant URL or join code, content, rights involved, the relationship between the complainant and rights holder, and requested action through this contact. Do not send full card numbers, security codes, or information unnecessary for the request. The operator will individually explain if proof of identity or rights ownership is required.

Depending on the complaint, the operator may temporarily hide content, suspend sales, request documentation, edit or delete content, provide buyers an alternative or refund, hold streamer distributions, or take other necessary measures. Users must help verify facts and provide rights documentation when submitted questions or materials are involved.
