Article 1 (Contract structure)

These terms apply between the Streetboardgame operator (“operator”) and streamers, VTubers, channel or streaming account operators, or other contracting parties using paid sales in LIVE streaming challenge mode (“streamers”). Users who only create or join free LIVE sessions do not need to agree to these terms or register for sales.

The operator provides and sells original high-resolution result image generation/download and paid support messages to viewers. Streamers are not the sellers; they license content and receive 70% of eligible revenue as contractual distributions.

Article 2 (Applications and consent records)

Streamers apply for the contract by reviewing the full terms on the web consent screen and agreeing. The operator retains the following contract evidence.

  • Version of agreed terms
  • Consent timestamp
  • IP address and device/browser information at consent
  • Stripe Connect account ID
  • Streaming account/channel IDs, names/business details, and other information needed for identity and authority verification

The contract is concluded when required identity verification, streaming account/channel management authority verification, Stripe Connect registration, and operator review are complete and paid sales are approved. Sales registration is by invitation and manual review; invitation codes may be used only by streamers under review and official staff. Current sales registration uses YouTube channel management authority verification.

Article 3 (Registration eligibility and warranties)

Streamers warrant that they have authority to manage the registered streaming account/channel, enter these terms, and license images and names. Obtain any agency, company, or co-operator approval beforehand.

Article 4 (License to use images and names)

Streamers grant the operator a free, non-exclusive license to use registered names, account/channel names, profile images, portraits, photos, logos, question text, and other materials for the following purposes and scope.

  • Creating, streaming, and running LIVE sessions; result screens; and generating free and high-quality result images
  • Providing original high-quality result images, buyer downloads, and re-download support
  • In-service notices, support, fraud investigations, and responses to rights infringements
  • Cropping, resizing, combining with text and decoration, and format conversion as necessary for the purposes above

The license is worldwide, for the contract duration and the period necessary for 30-day redelivery to existing buyers, refunds, and disputes. The operator will not use the streamer as if endorsing third-party products without prior consent.

Article 5 (Eligible services and prices)

Streamers select 480, 980, or 2,980 yen for original high-resolution result image generation/download. Paid support fees are 180, 480, 980, 1,980, or 2,980 yen. All fees are tax-inclusive viewer prices; changes after launch apply only to subsequent purchases.

The paid support message feature is not a donation or gift. It displays an optional message in LIVE chat in a color corresponding to the amount and notifies the streamer that support has arrived. You may purchase without entering a message. A purchase does not guarantee a reply, read-aloud or thanks, influence over the stream, special rights, or ongoing display.

Article 6 (Revenue distribution)

  1. Revenue eligible for sharing is sales of the original high-quality result image generation/download service and paid support messages for which the operator confirms completed payment. Result image sales and paid support message sales are separately aggregated in sales statements.
  2. Streamer distributions are 70% of gross eligible revenue including tax. Fractional amounts below 1 yen are rounded down per order.
  3. The operator bears card processing fees, operator-side Stripe Connect transfer/payout costs, and operator Connect account costs, while maintaining the stated streamer share of “70% of sales”. However, fees charged to streamers under their own bank or Stripe account agreements may be borne by streamers.
  4. Consumption tax, withholding tax, and other legally required deductions are handled in accordance with law and shown in the statement.
  5. Streamers are responsible for their own income tax, corporate tax, and other tax filings and payments.
  6. The operator does not guarantee LIVE viewer counts, paid support message counts or amounts, result image purchases, other sales, or minimum distributions.

Article 7 (Revenue finalization, holds, and payment)

  1. Result image and paid support revenue is held for 14 days after successful payment for refunds and fraud prevention.
  2. After the hold period, sales not subject to refund, payment cancellation, chargeback, misuse, or investigation of legal or terms violations are reflected in the confirmed balance.
  3. Once a month, no earlier than 14 days after the relevant month ends in Japan time, finalized balances are calculated. If the transferable amount, excluding amounts under refund or fraud review, is at least 5,000 yen, it is transferred to the streamer's Stripe Connect account.
  4. Finalized balances below 5,000 yen carry over to later months. Without refunds or similar adjustments, approximately 7,143 yen in total sales is needed to reach 5,000 yen.
  5. Transfers from the operator to Stripe Connect and payouts from Stripe Connect to the streamer's bank account are separate processes. Bank arrival dates depend on the streamer's Stripe payout schedule, review, holidays, incomplete information, legal compliance, and other factors.

Article 8 (Refunds, chargebacks, and offsets)

  1. If a buyer refund, payment cancellation, chargeback, fraud, or legally required tax/deduction occurs, the corresponding streamer distribution is held or canceled.
  2. The operator may refund buyers without waiting for cancellation or recovery of streamer distributions. If already transferred to the streamer, the corresponding amount may be offset against future balances; repayment may be requested if the balance is insufficient.
  3. A paid support message being hidden due to the terms, law, safety, report handling, or streamer moderation does not automatically make its order eligible for a refund or cancellation of distribution. However, distributions may be held if there is fraud, a streamer-involved terms violation, rights infringement, or other circumstances requiring investigation.
  4. If an operator-side error prevents support acceptance, LIVE chat display, or streamer notification, cannot be restored or redelivered, and the buyer is refunded, no distribution arises for that order.
  5. The operator separates result image and paid support revenue and reasonably lists orders, reasons, and amounts affected by refunds, chargebacks, or offsets in sales statements.

Article 9 (Identity verification and fraud prevention)

Streamers cooperate with Stripe Connect identity verification, payment destination registration, and required documentation. If impersonation, rights infringement, payment fraud, vote manipulation, leaks of invitation codes or streamer/staff URLs, or other concerns arise, the operator may suspend invitations, LIVE sessions, sales, distributions, or accounts until investigation ends. Paid support messages are published in LIVE chat after payment and may be hidden by the streamer or operator for safety or operational needs. Streamers are not obliged to reply, read messages aloud, or express thanks.

Article 10 (Prohibited content and rights infringements)

StreamersContent and Likeness Guidelineswill be followed. If a third party makes a claim, cooperate with providing rights documentation, hiding content, withholding sales, and assisting buyers.

Article 11 (Contract duration and termination)

The contract takes effect on approval and continues until terminated by the operator or streamer. New paid services stop after termination, but clauses necessary for existing orders, refunds, chargebacks, accounting, taxes, and dispute handling remain effective. Unpaid balances are handled under these terms, including hold periods, minimum transfers, and offsets.

Article 12 (Terms changes and governing law)

Changes to revenue share, transfer/payout conditions, licensing scope, or other important matters are notified before taking effect, with renewed consent requested when necessary. These terms are governed by Japanese law. To the extent legally permitted, Maebashi District Court or Maebashi Summary Court has exclusive agreed jurisdiction in the first instance.
