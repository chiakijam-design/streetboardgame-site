This is a simplified summary. The full provisions below take precedence for the formal conditions for minors.

1. Scope

This policy applies to users under 18. Users under 18 must show their legal representative, such as a parent or guardian (hereafter “guardian”), this policy andTermsHave your guardian review it and obtain their consent before using this service.

If you are under 18, show this page to your guardian and get consent before using the service. Users aged 10–12 must use it with a guardian.

2. Quizzes and the understanding board

Finishing adds your display name and score to the board automatically.

Retry on this device to improve your best score. Higher scores come first; ties keep first-listed order.

Your earlier private result stays private. Finishing a retry automatically lists your display name and that score.

People with this quiz URL can see display names and scores only. Individual answers are visible only to the creator and the person who answered.

Do not enter real names, school names, addresses, phone numbers, or other information you would not want made public.

3. Creating questions and submitting candidates for publication

Question text and choices submitted with consent for publication review may be published for other users in the shared question library after operator review and editing. Questions not submitted for publication review will not be published for this purpose.

Text resembling real names, school names, social media IDs, phone numbers, or addresses is subject to automatic detection. Sexual content, bullying, appearance-based attacks, discriminatory language, and other safety concerns are reviewed and may be rejected. Even after acceptance, reported questions are temporarily hidden and re-reviewed.

4. External services and precedence of provisions

This service is not provided, sponsored, or endorsed by YouTube, Google, Instagram, LINE, X, other external services, or their affiliates. When sharing content to external services, follow their terms, age requirements, and rights rules.
