Simple summary for children

  • If you are under 18, show this page to your guardian and get consent before using the service. Users aged 10–12 must use it with a guardian.
  • Do not enter real names, school names, addresses, phone numbers, or other information you would not want made public.
  • Consult your guardian before using paid features, and never use someone else's card without permission.
  • Follow the age rules of the streaming service you use. Generally, you must be at least 16 to start streaming on YouTube.
  • Minors need clear parental consent to register as streamers for sales and revenue receipt. Sales registration is not needed for free LIVE use alone.

This is a simplified summary. The full provisions below take precedence for the formal conditions for minors.

1. Scope

This policy applies to users under 18. Users under 18 must show their legal representative, such as a parent or guardian (hereafter “guardian”), this policy andTermsHave your guardian review it and obtain their consent before using this service.

This service is designed primarily for secondary-school students aged 13–18, secondarily for university and vocational students aged 19–20, and on a restricted basis for elementary-school children aged 10–12. These are service audience categories and do not change legal definitions of adulthood or minority. Users aged 10–12 should use the service under parental supervision. Even users aged 18 or older must follow any additional conditions imposed by payment methods, streaming services, agencies, schools, or household rules.

2. Standard mode and the understanding board

In regular mode, participants answer 10 creator-made questions through a joining URL. Appearing on the understanding board is optional. Participants can guess again without posting their current score, then choose whether to post. If posted, their display name, score, and chosen or self-written comment are shown to other participants in the same quiz. Posting without a comment is also possible.

Do not enter real names, school names, social media IDs, phone numbers, addresses, current locations, or other identifying or tracking information in display names, questions, choices, comments, or other fields. The operator automatically detects text resembling personal information, but may not detect everything.

3. Creating questions and submitting candidates for publication

Users can create or edit question text and 5 choices. If “Let friends and others use this quiz” remains ON, created or edited content is submitted to the operator as a publication candidate. The operator may edit and publish it to other users in the shared question library for standard and LIVE modes. Uncheck the box before submission if you do not want to submit a candidate. You can still create your quiz with the box unchecked.

Text resembling real names, school names, social media IDs, phone numbers, or addresses is subject to automatic detection. Sexual content, bullying, appearance-based attacks, discriminatory language, and other safety concerns are reviewed and may be rejected. Even after acceptance, reported questions are temporarily hidden and re-reviewed.

4. Using LIVE mode

In LIVE mode, streamers and viewers answer the same questions simultaneously. The streamer's answer is revealed after the streamer closes voting. No streamer registration is needed to create or join free LIVE sessions.

If you use Instagram, YouTube, or other streaming services, also follow each service's terms on age, guardian consent, visibility, and safety. Generally, you must be at least 16 to start a YouTube live stream. Users under 16 appearing in YouTube live streams must meet YouTube's conditions, such as having an adult visible on screen and participating in the stream to an equal or greater extent.

Streamer and staff URLs are secret URLs that control LIVE sessions. Do not confuse them with viewer joining URLs or 6-digit codes, and do not share them with unauthorized people. During streams, do not mention or show names, ages, addresses, schools, phone numbers, or other personal information in the background.

5. Purchasing paid features

Before applying for original high-resolution result image generation/download or the support feature, minors must show their parent or guardian the purchase details and tax-inclusive amount and obtain consent. Do not use a parent's or other person's card without permission. Support is a paid feature, not a donation or gift.

The purchase screen shows fees, service details, delivery completion, download deadlines, and refund conditions. The statutory cooling-off system does not apply to online sales. This policy does not limit minors' cancellation rights or other rights granted by law. For refunds and cancellations,Refund and cancellation policywill be followed.

6. Registration of streamers selling paid services

To sell result images or accept support and receive distributions, streamer registration, verification of streaming account or channel management authority,Revenue Sharing Terms for LIVE StreamersConsent, Stripe Connect identity verification, and operator review are required. If you only use free LIVE sessions, neither sales registration nor agreement to the Revenue Sharing Terms is required.

Users under 18 registering as streamers who sell paid services need explicit guardian consent. Follow Stripe's age and identity verification requirements and complete the procedures Stripe specifies, including having a legal guardian own the Stripe account when required. Obtain any required approval from agencies, co-operators, or other rights holders in advance.

7. Images, likenesses, and copyrighted works

Do not register or publish images of others, illustrations, logos, video clips, or other works made by others without permission from the people or rights holders involved. Images showing identifiable minors require consent from both the minor and guardian. Images showing minors in sexual or dangerous situations, including nudity, underwear, swimwear, bathing, or changing clothes, cannot be registered. Details are inContent and Likeness GuidelinesPlease follow these conditions.

8. False age or consent information and suspension

You must not make false declarations about your age, parental consent, authority to manage streaming accounts or channels, permission to use images or copyrighted works, or other matters. If false declarations, rights infringements, or safety issues are discovered, the operator may hide questions or images, suspend use or sales, hold distributions, or take other necessary measures.

9. Requests from guardians

Guardians concerned about a minor's use, purchases, questions or comments, publication of images or display names, or streamer registration mayContact formYou may request suspension, deletion, refund review, or other action through this contact. We will verify identity, the guardian relationship, and relevant information and respond under applicable laws and policies.

10. External services and precedence of provisions

YouTube, Instagram, Stripe, and other external services have their own terms of use, age requirements, and privacy policies. If these provisions differ from external service conditions, follow the stricter age, consent, and safety requirements. These provisions andTermsIf other individual conditions conflict, the conditions specified for that feature and applicable laws take precedence.
