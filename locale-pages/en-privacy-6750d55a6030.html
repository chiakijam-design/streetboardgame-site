1. Scope and business operator

This policy applies to how user information is handled in the free quiz “Know Me Quiz” and its related features.

The business handling personal information is Streetboardgame operator Chiaki Hirakawa (the “operator”). In this policy, “user information” means information collected, generated, or stored in connection with this service; “personal information” means personal information as defined in Article 2, Paragraph 1 of Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

2. Information collected

The operator collects user-entered or submitted information and information automatically generated during use within the following scope.

  • Let everyone try:Creator and display names; questions, choices, answers and results; best score; creation, answer, listing and update times
  • Custom question publication submissions:Question text, choices, source mode, submission timestamp, automatically detected review flags, and anti-abuse IP hashes for submissions users consented to through the optional checkbox
  • Report a question:The reported question, reason for reporting, optional details, report date and time, and an IP hash to prevent duplicate or fraudulent reports
  • Inquiries and access:Names, email addresses, inquiry contents, IP addresses, cookies, viewed pages, referrers, device information, and error logs

We use IP address hashes to detect abuse and duplicates and handle them securely.

My questions

Confirming an edit automatically saves the question and choices in this browser. The latest 30 questions are kept; older questions and their versions are replaced when the limit is exceeded.

Only question text and choices are saved in this browser. Choose the answer each time. Others using this browser can see them. Clearing site data removes them; they do not transfer to another device or browser.

When you create and share a quiz, its selected questions are saved on the server and shown to respondents.

4. Visibility and sharing with other users

Question text and choices submitted with consent for publication review may be published for other users in the shared question library after operator review and editing. Questions not submitted for publication review will not be published for this purpose.

  • Understanding board:

    Finishing adds your display name and score to the board automatically.

    Retry on this device to improve your best score. Higher scores come first; ties keep first-listed order.

    Your earlier private result stays private. Finishing a retry automatically lists your display name and that score.

    People with this quiz URL can see display names and scores only. Individual answers are visible only to the creator and the person who answered.

  • Organizer answer management:Answers to individual questions are displayed to the creator holding the secret organizer URL. The organizer URL is not disclosed to respondents.

If strings resembling real names, school names, social media IDs, phone numbers, or addresses are detected in question text or choices, the publication candidate is not saved and the sender is asked to correct it. Possible sexual content, bullying, appearance attacks, or discrimination are saved with review flags for the operator to check.

6. Disclosure to third parties

The operator does not provide personal data to third parties except in the following cases.

  • When publishing understanding boards, custom questions, or other information at the person's direction or with their consent
  • When necessary to outsource operations, support, or analytics
  • Disclosure in connection with a merger, business transfer, or other business succession
  • When receiving a lawful request under law from courts, police, tax offices, or other public authorities
  • When necessary to protect life, body, or property or the rights or safety of users or third parties and obtaining consent is difficult, or in other cases permitted by law

7. Statistical information

The operator may use non-identifying aggregate play counts, usage, error counts, and other statistics to improve quality, consider participation limits, prevent fraud, and summarize question popularity. Display names, individual answers, and IP addresses are excluded or aggregated, and no processing is performed to re-identify people.

9. Cookies and analytics

This service uses cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, and similar technologies to maintain features, resume progress, understand usage, and improve the service. You may disable or delete cookies and site data in your browser, but some features such as resuming progress or participant authentication may become unavailable.

Google Analytics 4 collects page views, referrers, device and browser information, and interaction events. We do not send names, email addresses, quiz questions, or answers as GA4 event data. See the Google Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles information.

Microsoft Clarity collects pages viewed, device and browser information, clicks, scrolling, and other interactions. We use heatmaps and session replays to improve usability. We disable Clarity cookies and strictly mask all on-screen text so that names, email addresses, questions, and answers are not sent to Clarity. For details, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Google AdSense

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies based on visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners personalize ads. You can disable personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

10. Security management

If a personal data breach or possible breach is identified, we stop access, preserve evidence, determine the impact, and contact contractors. Where legally required, we report to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission and notify affected individuals.

11. Disclosure, correction, suspension, deletion, etc.

Use the Contact form to request information about why we hold your personal data, access to it, corrections, additions, deletion, or an end to its use or disclosure to third parties. You can also submit other requests provided for by law. We will confirm the data concerned, your request, and your contact details, verify your identity as needed, and respond in accordance with the law.

We may be unable to fulfill all or part of requests concerning transaction records legally required to be retained, records needed to protect your or others' rights, or information whose immediate deletion during an ongoing game would seriously affect other participants. We will explain the reasons to the extent permitted by law.

12. Minors

If you are under 18, ask your guardian to review the Minor use policy and this policy and obtain their consent before playing. Children aged 10 to 12 should use the service under a guardian’s supervision. Keep real names, school names, social media handles, phone numbers, addresses, and other identifying or tracking information out of display names, questions, and answer choices.

Guardians can use the Contact form to request access to, correction of, suspension of use of, or deletion of a child’s information. We will verify that the request comes from the child or their guardian before acting.

13. Amendments and governing law

We will announce important changes and their effective dates on this site in advance. We will seek fresh consent when legally required, including for changes to purposes of use, visibility, disclosure to third parties, or transfers abroad. Japanese law governs this policy. The court specified in the Terms has jurisdiction in the first instance.

14. Contact

For questions or complaints about this policy, the handling of personal data, or requests for disclosure, contact the Streetboardgame operator through the Contact form.
