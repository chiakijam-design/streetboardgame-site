This is a simplified summary. The full Privacy Policy below takes precedence for details of information handling.

1. Scope and business operator

This policy applies to the handling of user information in Streetboardgame's free standard quiz and related features.

The business handling personal information is Streetboardgame operator Tomoaki Hirakawa (the “operator”). In this policy, “user information” means information collected, generated, or stored in connection with this service; “personal information” means personal information as defined in Article 2, Paragraph 1 of Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

2. Information collected

The operator collects user-entered or submitted information and information automatically generated during use within the following scope.

  • Let everyone try:Creator name, challenger-entered understanding board display name, selected questions, answers, number of matched questions, automatically generated titles and answer review reports, board publication consent timestamp, creation and answer timestamps
  • Custom question publication submissions:Question text, choices, source mode, submission timestamp, automatically detected review flags, and anti-abuse IP hashes for submissions users consented to through the optional checkbox
  • Report a question:The reported question, reason for reporting, optional details, report date and time, and an IP hash to prevent duplicate or fraudulent reports
  • Inquiries and access:Names, email addresses, inquiry contents, IP addresses, cookies, viewed pages, referrers, device information, and error logs

Card numbers, security codes, Google passwords, Google access tokens, and refresh tokens are not stored in the operator's database. IP hashes do not immediately reveal a person's identity but are treated as subject to security management because they are used to detect duplicates and misuse.

If information is used beyond purposes reasonably related to those stated at collection, renewed consent is obtained unless the use is based on law.

Answer review reports are generated automatically within this service from matching and differing answers. Answers are not currently sent to external AI services to generate these reports.

The operator does not provide user information for AI training of third-party products/services or sell or license personal information or non-identifying processed statistics. Any future change requires not only a policy update but legally required explanations and consent.

4. Visibility and sharing with other users

Question text and choices submitted with consent for publication review may be published for other users in the shared question library after operator review and editing. Questions not submitted for publication review will not be published for this purpose.

  • Understanding board:Listing is optional. Only the display name and number of matching answers of those who consent are shown to participants in the same quiz, without comments. Those who do not opt in will not appear on the understanding board.
  • Organizer answer management:Answers to individual questions are displayed to the creator holding the secret organizer URL. The organizer URL is not disclosed to respondents.

If strings resembling real names, school names, social media IDs, phone numbers, or addresses are detected in question text or choices, the publication candidate is not saved and the sender is asked to correct it. Possible sexual content, bullying, appearance attacks, or discrimination are saved with review flags for the operator to check.

6. Disclosure to third parties

The operator does not provide personal data to third parties except in the following cases.

  • When publishing understanding boards, custom questions, or other information at the person's direction or with their consent
  • When needed to outsource operations, payments, inquiry handling, analytics, or other work for this service
  • Disclosure in connection with a merger, business transfer, or other business succession
  • When receiving a lawful request under law from courts, police, tax offices, or other public authorities
  • When necessary to protect life, body, or property or the rights or safety of users or third parties and obtaining consent is difficult, or in other cases permitted by law

7. Statistical information

The operator may use non-identifying aggregate play counts, usage, error counts, and other statistics to improve quality, consider participation limits, prevent fraud, and summarize question popularity. Display names, individual answers, and IP addresses are excluded or aggregated, and no processing is performed to re-identify people.
