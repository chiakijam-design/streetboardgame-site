1. Basic policy

This policy applies to the original result image generation/download service and paid support message feature provided to buyers by the Streetboardgame operator in LIVE mode. The operator is the seller and service provider to viewers; the streamer is not the seller.

The result image service generates a 2,160×2,700px high-resolution image after payment and makes it downloadable for 30 days after payment. Delivery is complete when the download link becomes available.

The paid support message feature is not a donation or gift; it is use of a paid feature whose acceptance completes when payment finishes. An optional message is then displayed in LIVE chat in the color for the selected amount, and the streamer is notified of the support. You may use it without entering a message. Goods, result images, replies, read-alouds or thanks, influence over the stream, special rights, and ongoing display are not provided or guaranteed.

2. Cancellation before order confirmation

You may stop checkout before confirming an order on Stripe's purchase screen. Once an order is confirmed and payment completes, the cooling-off system does not apply to distance sales, and the following conditions apply.

3. Full refunds

  • Duplicate payment:When an unintended duplicate payment for the same result image service or paid support message feature is confirmed
  • Result image generation issues:An operator-side error prevents generating a high-resolution result image, and regeneration or reasonable alternatives cannot resolve it
  • Result image delivery issues:An operator-side error prevents downloading a generated image, and URL reissue, access recovery, or redelivery cannot resolve it
  • Paid support message issues:Payment was made, but an operator-side error prevented recording support, displaying the message in LIVE chat, or notifying the streamer, and restoration or redelivery is impossible

4. Re-provision or case-by-case decisions

In the following cases, we check purchase times, generation/download records, support acceptance/display/notification records, and reports. We first attempt regeneration, URL reissue, permission recovery, or other restoration or redelivery, and consider a refund if the issue cannot be resolved.

  • You discover an accidental purchase before downloading the high-resolution result image even once
  • There is a clear operator-side discrepancy between the purchase screen description or free preview and the generated result
  • You cannot return to the collection screen or obtain a download link after payment
  • Support acceptance was recorded, but an operator-side error prevents confirmation that display or streamer notification was completed

Accidental purchases for buyer-related reasons do not guarantee a refund. Contact us promptly after discovering the issue.

5. Cases generally not eligible for refunds

  • Buyer preferences such as an image differing from expectations or no longer being needed after a high-quality image conforming to the contract has been generated and made available for normal download
  • Inability to obtain the image due to insufficient device storage, connection issues, saving actions, or expiration of the 30-day deadline
  • Errors in names or other content entered by the buyer, except operator-side display errors
  • Buyer-related reasons after payment and support acceptance, such as choosing the wrong amount, forgetting or mistyping a message, or changing their mind
  • No reply, read-aloud or thanks from the streamer, no influence on the stream, or a shorter display time as chat progresses
  • A support message being hidden because of terms violations, laws, safety, reports, or streamer moderation
  • When a paid support message feature is purchased without entering a message and support acceptance and notification to the streamer complete normally
  • Issues caused solely by the streamer's or buyer's device, streaming service, connection, or other circumstances outside the operator's control

6. Alternative image processing

If the image conversion service is busy, restricted, or unavailable, the operator may generate the high-resolution result image using the original unconverted image rather than immediately fail the purchase. If that image meets the purchase details, this alone is not grounds for a refund. If regeneration or alternative delivery still cannot meet those details, a full refund will be issued.

7. Re-downloading and restoring purchase access

Generated high-resolution result images can be downloaded again from the collection screen for 30 days after payment. If you lose that screen, you can recover purchase access using your order number and email address, or contact support for verification. After the 30-day deadline, redelivery and refunds are unavailable except for operator-side errors or when required by law.

8. How to request a refund

Refund/cancellation request formSubmit your order email address, order number, payment date/time, amount, service, LIVE name or join code, and reason through this contact. Include a Stripe receipt or payment identifier if available. Do not send full card numbers, security codes, or identity documents.

9. Refund methods and timing

If a refund is approved, the full order amount is generally refunded to the card used for payment. The buyer's refund is not reduced even if paid support revenue was eligible for streamer distribution. After the operator processes a Stripe refund, the time until it appears on the card statement depends on the card issuer.

10. Misuse and chargebacks

For unrecognized payments, contact your card issuer and the operator promptly. For problems with recognized payments, first use the refund/cancellation request form to avoid duplicate handling. Fraud, chargebacks, or payment cancellations cause related streamer distributions to be held, canceled, or offset. This does not obstruct buyers' or cardholders' legitimate exercise of rights.

11. Relationship with laws

Where refunds, cancellation, or similar action are legally required for nonconformity, cancellation by minors, or other reasons, applicable law takes precedence over this policy.

Before applying:Before confirming your order, review the service, tax-inclusive fee, delivery completion point, download deadline, and refund conditions on the final confirmation screen. Paid support messages do not purchase replies or read-aloud responses.
